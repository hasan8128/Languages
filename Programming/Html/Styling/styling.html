Oil Production Statistics

Recent Statistics

U.S. crude oil production reached a record-breaking
average of 13.6 million barrels per day in 2025,
driven by ongoing efficiency gains and shale drilling
in the Permian Basin.



Production History

The world holds roughly 1.57 to 1.76 trillion barrels
of proven reserves, overwhelmingly dominated by Venezuela
(the largest overall but costly to extract) and Saudi Arabia
(the largest accessible reserves), with OPEC members controlling
nearly 80% of the total.
